Formula

X% of Y = (X ÷ 100) × Y
Percentage Change = ((New − Old) ÷ Old) × 100

Example

Q: What is 20% of 500?
A: (20 ÷ 100) × 500 = 100

FAQs

How do I calculate a percentage increase?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value, then multiply by 100. A positive result means an increase, a negative result means a decrease.

Can percentages be more than 100%?

Yes. Percentages above 100% simply mean the value is more than double (or more) of the base value.
