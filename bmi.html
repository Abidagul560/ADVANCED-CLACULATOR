How to Use

  1. Enter your weight in kilograms.
  2. Enter your height in centimeters.
  3. Click Calculate BMI to see your result and category.

Formula

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ [Height (m)]²

Example

Q: Weight = 70 kg, Height = 175 cm (1.75 m)
A: BMI = 70 ÷ (1.75 × 1.75) = 22.9 (Normal weight)

BMI Categories

  • Below 18.5 — Underweight
  • 18.5 – 24.9 — Normal weight
  • 25 – 29.9 — Overweight
  • 30 and above — Obese

FAQs

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

BMI is a general screening tool and doesn't account for muscle mass, bone density, or body composition. It should not replace professional medical advice.

Does this work for children?

This calculator uses the standard adult BMI formula. Children and teens require age- and gender-specific BMI charts.

What units does this calculator use?

Weight in kilograms and height in centimeters.
