Formula

Age = Current Date − Date of Birth (broken into years, months, days)

Example

Q: Born on 15 March 2000 — how old are they in 2026?
A: Approximately 26 years, 6 months (depending on today's date).

FAQs

Does this account for leap years?

Yes, the calculation is based on actual calendar dates, so leap years are automatically handled correctly.

Can I calculate age as of a future date?

This calculator computes age as of today's date. For a custom target date, the underlying logic can easily be adapted.

Is my birth date stored anywhere?

No. All calculations happen instantly in your browser — nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
