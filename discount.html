How to Use

  1. Enter the original price of the item.
  2. Enter the discount percentage.
  3. Click Calculate to see the final price and your savings.

Formula

Discount Amount = Original Price × (Discount % ÷ 100)
Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Example

Q: Original Price = 2000, Discount = 25%
A: Discount = 500 → Final Price = 1500

FAQs

How do I calculate the final price after multiple discounts?

Apply discounts one after another — calculate the first discount, then apply the second discount to the already-reduced price (not the original).

Does this include tax?

No, this calculator only computes the discount. Any applicable tax should be calculated separately on the final price.

Can I use this for percentage-off coupons?

Yes, simply enter the coupon's discount percentage and the item's original price.
